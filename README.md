# credit-analysis-report-JSW-
### JSW Steel Ltd. – Credit & Financial Statement Analysis (FY24–FY26)

This project involved analysing JSW Steel Ltd. from a lender’s perspective to evaluate its financial performance, creditworthiness, and ability to repay debt. I analysed financial statements and key ratios covering growth, liquidity, solvency, debt service coverage, and market valuation, along with the company’s credit ratings and Altman Z-Score.

Through this project, I learned how to interpret financial ratios beyond their numerical values, understand the impact of steel price fluctuations, import competition, and capital expenditure on financial performance, and assess the difference between profitability and actual debt repayment capacity. I also gained practical insights into how banks and credit analysts evaluate financial risks, identify credit strengths and warning signs, and make informed lending recommendations based on a company’s financial position.
